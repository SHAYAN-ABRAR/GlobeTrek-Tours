# GlobeTrek Tours — Travel Agency Landing Page

A travel-agency landing page written in **pure HTML and CSS**, with no frameworks. It covers the trip search, popular tours, a destination gallery, an embedded video and a newsletter sign-up.

## Sections

- **Hero:** "Explore Your Travel", with a trip search bar for destination, time of day and travel type (solo or family)
- **Our Popular Tours:** a highlights list and a *Read More* call to action
- **Choose Your Destination:** a gallery of Maldives, Indonesia, Sri Lanka, North America, Kashmir, Bangladesh and Bandarban
- **Why Choose Us:** handpicked hotels, world-class service and a best-price guarantee
- **"A Simple Perfect Place To Get Lost":** feature copy with an embedded YouTube video
- **Newsletter:** name and email sign-up
- **Footer** with brand message and social links

## Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
